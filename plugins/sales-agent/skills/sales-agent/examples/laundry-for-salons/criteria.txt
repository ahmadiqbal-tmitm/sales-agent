- is located in Dubai Marina, JLT (Jumeirah Lake Towers) or Business Bay;
- has 1 or 2 locations;
- is independent: not a spa, hotel spa, large chain or franchise;

Mark "no" if its address is clearly outside those areas, it has 3 or more locations, or it is a chain or spa.
